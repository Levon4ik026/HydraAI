## Fresh backtest from current-HEAD CI: standalone M5 family screen

Source: `eurusd_history_extended.csv`, Dukascopy EUR/USD M1 BID; 30,048 M1 rows, 6,008 complete M5 bars, 2 gap-separated segments. Coverage: 2026-10-07 04:31 UTC through 2026-10-28 01:19 UTC. The split is by observed calendar dates: TRAIN before 2026-10-20, VALIDATION 2026-10-20 through 2026-10-24, TEST from 2026-10-24. The three splits contain 3,688 / 1,152 / 1,168 M5 bars.

Execution and payout:
- 20 predeclared direction-specific strategy families × 6 expiries = 120 family/expiry variants.
- Signal from a completed M5 candle; entry at the next M5 open; expiry at the close of the Nth subsequent M5 bar (5/10/15/20/30/60 minutes).
- One active position per family/expiry/continuity segment; no overlapping positions within a variant.
- Any signal whose expiry crosses a data gap or chronological split boundary is excluded.
- Fixed hypothetical payout 92%: WIN +0.92u, LOSS -1u, DRAW 0u. ROI is net divided by all accepted one-unit stakes. Wilson intervals use decisive outcomes only.
- Dukascopy BID is not Pocket Option's executable quote stream; no platform quote mismatch, variable payout, spread or latency is modeled.

The strongest validation result by net among rows with at least 20 validation trades was `MEAN_REV_20_Z1.5_CALL`, 5-minute expiry. Its exact frozen rule is: using the prior 20 M5 closes, calculate the prior-close z-score against their rolling mean and population standard deviation; signal CALL when z <= -1.5 and the current completed M5 candle is bullish and closes above the prior close. Enter next M5 open; settle at the close of that entry candle.
