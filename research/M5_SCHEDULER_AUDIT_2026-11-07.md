# M5 research scheduler audit — 2026-11-07

Branch: `hydra/stabilization`  
Payout convention: **92% only** (+0.92 win, -1.00 loss, 0 draw).

## Finding

A code review of `research/reproduce_m5_bb_williams_adx.py` found two scheduler defects in the previous implementation:

1. **Expiry-close signal was skipped.** A position entered at the next M5 candle open and settled at that same candle's close. The previous loop advanced by two rows after a trade, skipping the expiry candle as a potential signal candle. A signal generated at that expiry close is valid and can enter the following candle without overlapping the previous position.
2. **Segment boundary was not explicit in the flattened rows.** The old scheduler could treat the first candle of a later continuity segment as the entry candle after a signal at the end of an earlier segment. This violates the no-gap-crossing rule.

## Correction committed

- Every signal row now carries its source `segment_id`.
- Entry is allowed only on the immediately following M5 candle, exactly five minutes later, in the same segment.
- After a trade settles at the entry candle close, the scheduler advances one row, so a signal on that close can be evaluated for entry on the next candle.
- Added regression tests for expiry-close entries, segment-boundary rejection, and missing M5 bucket rejection.

## Impact on previous results

The previously reported holdout counts (ADX 15: 48 trades; ADX 20: 39; ADX 25: 21) and all derived rates/net units are **withdrawn**. They were generated by the defective scheduler and must not be quoted as valid strategy performance. The corrected CI rerun is required before new metrics are published.

The broad 486,488-candle screening summary on `main` is a separate prior research output and was not generated by this short-sample script. Its reported combined-direction results are not invalidated by this specific correction, although the raw broad dataset and exact implementation are still unavailable in the working branch for independent reproduction.

## Safety / scope

No desktop application, production signal logic, or automated trading behavior was changed. This is research-only code on `hydra/stabilization`. The 92% payout convention remains fixed.
