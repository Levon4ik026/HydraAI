## Multi-year EURUSD backtest

Source: Dukascopy EURUSD M1 BID, 1,061,815 valid M1 rows, 209,934 complete M5 bars, 2,655 gap-separated segments. Coverage: 2024-01-01 22:00 UTC through 2026-11-08 23:58 UTC.

Chronological split used by the code:
- TRAIN: observed dates before 2025-09-17 — 125,989 M5 bars, 536 observed UTC trading dates.
- VALIDATION: 2025-09-17 through 2026-04-13 — 41,785 M5 bars, 179 observed UTC trading dates.
- TEST: from 2026-04-14 — 42,160 M5 bars, 179 observed UTC trading dates.

Execution: signal after a completed M5 candle; entry at the next M5 open; expiry at the close of the Nth subsequent M5 candle (5/10/15/20/30/60m candidates). Signals whose settlement crosses a chronological split boundary or data gap are excluded. One-position non-overlap is enforced independently per strategy/expiry/continuous segment. Fixed payout 92%: WIN +0.92u, LOSS -1u, DRAW 0u; decisive break-even WR 52.0833%. ROI is net divided by all accepted one-unit stakes.

The weak-signal inversion cohort was frozen on TRAIN only: at least 100 accepted trades, original decisive WR no higher than 47.9167%, and original net below zero. Thirteen strategy/expiry candidates were frozen. The inverse preserves the exact original event timestamps, entries, expiries and schedule; only CALL/PUT is flipped.

## Day-cluster bootstrap

5,000 resamples, fixed seed 20261109; UTC calendar days resampled within each split, including zero-trade days. This preserves within-day clustering but assumes day clusters are exchangeable and does not remove multi-day dependence.

For the four aggregate-positive candidates, all TEST 95% bootstrap CIs include zero:
- MOM_BREAK_12_CALL→PUT 15m: TEST net +8.76u; CI [-61.16, +77.24]u; P(resampled total net > 0)=59.9%.

## Reporting issues identified for correction

The split code itself uses `signal_day < d2` for VALIDATION and `signal_day >= d2` for TEST, and purges trades whose settlement crosses the boundary. Thus the actual validation interval ends 2026-04-13. Two report strings currently say “through 2026-04-14”, which is an inclusive-label off-by-one; the coverage tables label unique observed trading dates as “Calendar days”. The calculations do not overlap, but those labels should be corrected in the report generators.

The reports also call this “360 strategy/expiry combinations”. The correct discovery count is 120 distinct strategy/expiry variants; 360 is the number of variant/split result rows (120 × 3). This wording should be corrected in the bootstrap and payout-stress reports.

The dedicated fifth-wave workflow is configured to run on a push to `hydra/research-validation` when `research/fifth_wave_price_action_screen.py` changes. The current GitHub Actions connector exposes PR-triggered runs but not the push-triggered run list, so its run/artifact could not be independently retrieved in this check. No fifth-wave backtest result is claimed here.

## Verdict and next step

No confirmed robust edge. The four inversions that remain positive at 92% have tiny TRAIN/TEST margins, Wilson and day-bootstrap intervals spanning zero, quarterly instability, and negative TRAIN/TEST expectancy at 90% payout. The standalone and context screens also show substantial instability. Dukascopy BID is not Pocket Option executable pricing; spread, variable payout, platform quote differences, latency and rejected execution are not modeled. No live strategy is approved and no trades are executed.

Next research step: correct the split/report wording and multiple-testing count, then retrieve and audit the fifth-wave price-action discovery artifact without using TEST to select a candidate. Freeze any candidate on TRAIN/VALIDATION and reserve genuinely new prospective data for confirmation.
